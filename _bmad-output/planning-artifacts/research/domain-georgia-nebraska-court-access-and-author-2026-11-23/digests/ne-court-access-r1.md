# Digest — Nebraska court access (round 1)

Access date: 2026-11-23. Findings are claims with sources; no live query.

- C6 Nebraska Judicial Branch: JUSTICE statewide trial-court search and SCCALES appellate; free courthouse/law-library terminals; one-time internet search $17/30 records/3 days; subscriber $100/year + $2 per case detail; coverage county/district/juvenile and appellate. Source: https://nebraskajudicial.gov/e-services/case-information-eservices. Confidence: high. Class: access/pricing.
- C7 Court Records Search Policy: courthouse terminal; one-time and subscriber routes; formal Public Records Act §§84-712–84-712.09. Source: https://nebraskajudicial.gov/rules/administrative-policies-schedules/court-records-search-policy-nebraska-courts. Confidence: high. Class: access.
- C9 One-time terms: $17; 3 days; 30-result limit; "ARE NOT allowed to redistribute, republish, or retransmit." Source: https://www.nebraska.gov/justicecc/terms_and_conditions.html. Confidence: high. Class: permission/pricing.
- C10 Subscription agreement: non-transferable end-user license; copyright/limitation compliance; no tampering; audit. Source: https://www.ne.gov/subscriber/tac.html. Confidence: high. Class: permission.
- C8 Subscriber portal: $2 details; bulk $1,000/20,000; New Cases $1,200; Statewide Index $2,000; sealed-case index. Source: https://www.nebraska.gov/subscriber/. Confidence: high. Class: pricing.
- C11 2026 fee notice confirms $2 subscriber record, $1,000 bulk, $1,200 new-case index, $2,000 statewide index effective 2026-01-01. Source: PDF. Confidence: high. Class: pricing.
- C14 §43-2,108 juvenile records/confidential information not open without court order, subject to exceptions. Source: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=43-2,108. Confidence: high. Class: legal.
