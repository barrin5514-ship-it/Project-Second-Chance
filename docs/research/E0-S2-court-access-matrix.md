## Georgia detail

**Structure and gatekeepers.** The Judicial Council of Georgia/Administrative Office of the Courts publishes the E-Access list and directory; the Georgia Superior Court Clerks' Cooperative Authority (GSCCCA) is a legislatively created authority that describes statewide civil-case-filing and property/UCC indexes (exact case coverage is an inference, not established here); individual Superior Court clerks are the custodians of their courts' records; PeachCourt (GreenCourt) and re:SearchGA (Tyler) are the vendor access paths named by the Judicial Council. [C1][C2][C5][C12]

**Coverage and search.** E-Access is not a search engine: it is a court-by-court link table, and many links go to PeachCourt or re:SearchGA while some go to a vendor-choice page. It states: "You'll be redirected to the provider's website and must have an account to search court records." Which vendors support name-only search, record date ranges, and Magistrate/Municipal/Probate/Juvenile coverage were not established. [C1]

**Public/restricted.** Uniform Superior Court Rule 21 is a **Superior Court** rule, not a statewide statute for every Georgia court; a secondary reproduction quotes its public-access/sealing text and official verification is pending. A county clerk describes juvenile records as protected by O.C.G.A. § 15-11-704 ("strictly protected ... cannot be generally viewed by the public"); that verifies the clerk's description, not the statutory text. [C13][C15]

**Terms and automation.** GSCCCA's Terms of Use prohibit unauthorized use of "any robot, spider or other automated device" and state that its materials may not be "compiled, bundled, grouped, reproduced, shared, transmitted, transcribed, stored in a retrieval system" without express written permission; accounts cost $14.95/$29.95 per month, single-use activation is $5, and image access is print-only at $0.50/page. PeachCourt's terms document states a **2022-04-13 update** (though hosted under a 2025/01 path) and says: "Any attempt to collect data from PeachCourt in a manner that avoids billing is strictly prohibited and may result in criminal prosecution or civil action." PeachCourt charges $2.00 per document plus $0.50/page plus a convenience fee. These findings cover only the inspected sources; re:SearchGA/Tyler and county clerk sites were not reviewed. [C4][C3]

**What is not established:** whether any Georgia court or GSCCCA would grant written permission for a bounded read-only integration; whether an API exists for a permitted use; statewide or date-range coverage of a single vendor; complete fees for every court; deep-link terms; and retention/redistribution terms for each clerk. These remain **unknown** and require owner/vendor/court decisions before any live integration.

## Nebraska detail

**Structure and entry points.** The Nebraska Judicial Branch provides statewide trial-court searches from JUSTICE and appellate searches from SCCALES, with free public terminals in courthouses/law libraries and paid remote access through Nebraska.gov. The Court Records Search Policy describes the courthouse terminal, one-time internet search, subscriber access, and formal Public Records Act requests. [C6][C7]

**Coverage.** Trial courts are the 93 counties' county and district courts plus separate juvenile courts in Douglas, Lancaster and Sarpy; appellate coverage is Supreme Court and Court of Appeals. The page lists case summary, parties, offense information (criminal), financial information, register of actions, judge notes, and document images where available. It does not establish coverage of every specialized tribunal. [C6]

**Fees (documented units).** One-time: $17, up to 30 records, results available for three days; no-record searches are still charged [C6][C9]. Subscriber: $100 annual account fee; general searches free; viewing case details is $2; no additional charge for document images [C6]. Bulk: $1,000/month up to 20,000 cases, then **$2.00 per excess record**; New Case Index $1,200/month; Statewide Case Index $2,000/month [C8][C11]. The 1 January 2026 fee notice confirms the $2 subscriber record, $1,000 bulk, $1,200 new-case index and $2,000 statewide-index changes [C11]. The $17 one-time and $100 annual figures are separately listed on the official case-information page [C6]; prices are not guaranteed to remain unchanged.

**Terms and automation.** The **one-time** service terms prohibit redistribution, republishing or retransmission and permit personal viewing/printing for that service [C9]. The **subscriber agreement** grants a non-transferable end-user licence expressly for provided **software**, requires compliance with copyright/use limitations, prohibits tampering, and permits audits [C10]; it does not by itself establish a blanket data-display/redistribution right. Affirmative display permission for subscriber/bulk case data was **not established**, and bulk agreements were not retrieved [C8]. No public case-search API is documented. Trial Integrated eFiling is an attorney/subscription eFiling service, not a court-search API [C8].

**What is not established:** whether the owner can obtain a data-use agreement permitting application display of case data; the current bulk agreement's redistribution/retention terms (the linked agreement PDFs were not retrievable); record date ranges and specialized-tribunal coverage; whether one-time prices will remain unchanged; and whether any agency would authorize a read-only integration. These remain **unknown**.

## Unknowns and blockers

1. No written permission or API documentation was found for automated Georgia or Nebraska case search. Absence of a finding is not proof that no permission path exists.
2. No single statewide public Georgia case search was established in this bounded review; vendor coverage, search fields, record date ranges and court-type coverage vary/are unknown.
3. Nebraska's case search is paid and contracted; affirmative display/redistribution permission for subscriber/bulk data is not established; one-time terms separately prohibit redistribution.
4. Juvenile, sealed and other restricted records must be excluded; exact redaction/selection rules require qualified legal review. Nebraska §43-2,108 has specific categories/exceptions; broad exclusion is a product safety boundary, not a universal confidentiality claim.
5. Current complete fee schedules and bulk-data agreements need owner/vendor confirmation before any budget or integration decision. The $17/$100 figures are source-specific and not guaranteed indefinitely.
6. Georgia vendor/login and appellate search fields were not assessed; no access authorization is inferred from public visibility.
7. This matrix does not verify identity, case accuracy or completeness, and no name match is identity proof.
